01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

ABOUT ME.

I am Jhon Marck Nunga, often known as Koy, a struggling creative artist from Cubao, Quezon City, Philippines.

I am a multimedia practitioner passionate about photography, graphic design, and video editing. At the same time, I avidly pursue theatre arts through sound design, stage management, and technical operation. I endeavor at the intersection of visual arts and live performance, where digital precision meets the raw energy of the stage.

Currently, I am pursuing a degree in Multimedia Studies at the University of the Philippines Open University. I also served as the Publicity and Creatives Manager of The UP Repertory Company's 53rd Season, where I helped establish its season branding and visual identity, AGAW EK53NA.
